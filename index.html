02 — Day by Day

The Itinerary

N
Sept 3 (Thu) — Night Departure
San Antonio → Great Sand Dunes · ~10 hrs
⌄
🌙

Leave San Antonio · 8:00 PM

Rotate drivers every 2–3 hours. Gas up and grab snacks before leaving the city. Plan a fatigue-safe overnight drive.

1
Sept 4 (Fri) — Dunes + Colorado Springs
Great Sand Dunes → Colorado Springs
⌄
🏜️

Great Sand Dunes Must

Climb High Dune for the panoramic shot. If Medano Creek is flowing, wade at the base.

🪨

Garden of the Gods Must

Free entry. Red sandstone formations with Pikes Peak behind them; late afternoon light is excellent.

⛰️

Pikes Peak Highway

Drive to the summit. Expect dramatically colder conditions at elevation; pack a real warm layer.

🏨

Overnight — Colorado Springs

2 rooms planned.

2
Sept 5 (Sat) — Rocky Mountains
Colorado Springs → RMNP → Glenwood Springs
⌄
🏔️

Trail Ridge Road — RMNP Must

Rock Cut → Alpine Visitor Center → Medicine Bow Curve → Milner Pass → Lava Cliffs → Gore Range Overlook → Farview Curve → Holzwarth. Check current access and timed-entry requirements before departure.

🏞️

Bear Lake Loop

Easy classic lake loop; keep it optional if the day is running long.

🏞️

Twin Lakes Must

Turquoise water with high peaks behind it — a strong photo stop.

🏔️

Independence Pass

Continental Divide crossing. Stop at the summit only if the pass is open and conditions are good.

🏨

Overnight — Glenwood Springs

Convenient base for the hot springs and the next morning's Aspen/Maroon Bells plan.

3
Sept 6 (Sun) — Aspen, Telluride, Ouray
Glenwood → Maroon Bells → Aspen → Telluride → Ouray
⌄
🏔️

Maroon Bells Must

Plan around the current access/shuttle rules and reserve ahead when required. Early morning gives the best light.

🚠

Telluride — Free Gondola + Waterfalls Must

Ride the gondola to Mountain Village and explore the town. Bridal Veil Falls and Bear Creek Falls are options depending on energy/time.

🏔️

Ouray — Box Canyon + Hot Springs Must

Compact scenic stop followed by a relaxing evening in the mountain town.

4
Sept 7 (Mon) — Million Dollar Highway + Home
Ouray → Silverton → Durango → San Antonio
⌄
🛣️

Million Dollar Highway Must

Ouray → Silverton via US-550. Dramatic mountain road; drive conservatively and check conditions.

🏘️

Silverton

Historic mining town. Walk Blair Street and stretch before the long drive.

🚂

Durango — Narrow Gauge Railroad

Optional splurge; don't combine a full train excursion with an unsafe rushed return drive.

Drive home

Durango → San Antonio via US-160 / I-40 / I-25. Build in driver swaps and real rest breaks.

08 — Get Ready

Weather & Packing

September mountain weather can swing quickly. Pack for warm valleys and cold, windy summits.

Clothing

Layer system

Moisture-wicking base · fleece · wind/rain shell · warm summit layer · beanie · gloves · hiking shoes.

Must bring

Trip essentials

Permits/reservations · park pass if useful · sunscreen · water · cooler/snacks · offline maps.

Vehicle

Road-trip kit

Emergency blanket · first-aid kit · trash bags · chargers · spare water · tire/roadside basics.

Mountain rule

Start early

Front-load the scenic stops. Afternoon storms and changing mountain conditions can affect passes and high-elevation hikes.

Altitude

Hydrate & pace

Give everyone time to adjust. Headache, dizziness or unusual fatigue are reasons to slow down and reassess.

Navigation

Use live routing

Open the Google Maps buttons on travel day for current ETA and road conditions; don't rely on the static table for live traffic.
